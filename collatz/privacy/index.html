MrCollatz Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

MrCollatz does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • It does not connect to the internet. The only links in the app (the photo credit and license in Settings) open in your web browser when you tap them.
  • Your settings and a count of the numbers you have tested are saved on your device only, and are deleted when you delete the app.

If you have questions, contact support@44consulting.net.
