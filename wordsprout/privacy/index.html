Wordsprout Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

Wordsprout does not collect any information about you.

What stays on your phone

To keep your game going, Wordsprout saves a few things on your device only: which puzzle you're on, your puzzle order, your stats and solve times, when you last used hints, and whether Easy mode is on. This never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No network connection: the app works entirely offline and sends nothing anywhere.
  • No data is shared with or sold to anyone.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@44consulting.net.
