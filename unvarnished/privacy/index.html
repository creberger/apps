Unvarnished Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Unvarnished does not collect any information about you.

  • There are no accounts, no sign-in and no ads.
  • The app doesn't use analytics, tracking or any third-party services.
  • The app doesn't connect to the internet. All the paintings and questions are built into the app.
  • Your progress, meaning which paintings you've played and which you've acquired for your collection, is stored only on your device. It is deleted when you delete the app.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

If you have questions, contact support@44consulting.net.
