How it works

Each painting zooms into five details. For each detail you get three statements, and only one is true. Pick wrong and that statement greys out, so you keep looking until you find the truth. Every answer explains why it's true or false, so even a wrong guess teaches you something. The questions get harder as you go.

Get all five right on the first try and the painting is acquired for Your Collection, along with what you learned about it.

Common questions

How many paintings are there?
100, from the 1290s to 1930, in eight categories: Renaissance, Golden Age & Baroque, Rococo & Revolution, Romantics & Realists, Impressionism, After Impressionism, Modern, and Asia & Antiquity.

Where do the images come from?
Every image is a public-domain reproduction from the Metropolitan Museum of Art, the Cleveland Museum of Art and Wikimedia Commons.

Do I need an internet connection?
No. All the paintings and questions are built into the app.

How do I reset my collection?
Delete the app and install it again. Your progress is stored only on your device. See the privacy policy.

A statement looks wrong.
Please email us with the painting and the statement, and we'll check it.
