Spellfall Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Spellfall is a word game about spelling falling words. It is designed to respect your privacy completely.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps a few things on your own device so the game remembers them between plays: your best score for each language and word length, and your language and sound settings. This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party code that collects data, and no information is shared with anyone.

Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
