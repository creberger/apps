How to play

Pick a word length (4 to 8 letters) and tap Play. Words fall down the screen with some letters missing. Eight candidate letters sit along the bottom. Swipe from anywhere on the screen toward the letter you think fills the gap: the arrow turns green if you're right and red if not. Longer words have more than one gap, which you fill from left to right. When more than one word is falling, you always answer the lowest one.

A round is 10 words. The counter at the top shows how many have been spelled or have landed. Wrong swipes don't end the round, but they break your streak, and a word that reaches the bottom counts as missed. Words fall faster and more of them fall at once as you go. When the round ends you'll see your score, accuracy, words spelled first try, best streak and time.

Unlocking longer words. You start with 4 and 5 letter words. Spell 6 of the 10 words in a round at your longest open length to unlock the next one.

Tricky words. Words you miss, or get wrong before getting right, are remembered. When there are some at your chosen length, a Practice tricky words button appears under Play. A word leaves the list when you spell it first time.

Calm mode. Slower words, one at a time, each read aloud when you spell it. Calm and practice rounds don't change your scores, statistics or unlocks.

Levels. Every word you spell counts toward your level, and the background changes as you level up.

Common questions

There's no sound.
Check the speaker button on the main screen, and make sure your iPhone's silent switch is off and the volume is up.

How do I reset my scores?
Delete the app and install it again. Your scores are stored only on your device.

A word is missing or looks wrong.
Please email us and tell us which one.
