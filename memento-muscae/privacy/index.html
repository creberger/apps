Memento Muscae Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Memento Muscae does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • It does not connect to the internet, and it contains no third-party code that collects data.
  • Your high scores, difficulty and sound settings, and which kinds of fly you have swatted in the Fly Guide are saved on your device only, and are deleted when you delete the app.
  • Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

If you have questions, contact support@44consulting.net.
