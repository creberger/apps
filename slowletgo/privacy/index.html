SlowLetGo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

SlowLetGo does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.
  • The photo you choose stays on your device. SlowLetGo keeps one fading copy of it on your iPhone, and that copy is never uploaded or shared.
  • SlowLetGo asks for access to your Photos library only to delete the original photo when a fade begins. iOS asks you to confirm before anything is deleted.
  • Your sound setting and the fading copy are saved on your device only, and are deleted when you delete the app.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

If you have questions, contact support@44consulting.net.
