Burn Window Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Burn Window does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • It does not connect to the internet, and it contains no third-party code that collects data.
  • Your best result on each level, your difficulty setting and whether sound is on are saved on your device only, and are deleted when you delete the app.
  • Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

If you have questions, contact support@44consulting.net.
