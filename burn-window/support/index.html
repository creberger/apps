How to play

Swipe anywhere on the screen to fire your engine. The ship turns to face the direction you swipe, and a longer swipe gives more thrust. Keep your finger down to keep burning, and lift it to stop. Tap Watch a demo on the main menu to see it in action.

Swipe along the green tick next to your ship to speed up, and along the red tick to slow down. The blue line shows where you'll go if you stop burning.

Common questions

When should I leave for the next planet?
Wait for the panel at the top to say Burn now, then swipe along the green tick. That's the burn window: the moment that gets you there with the least fuel. If you miss it, tap the skip button to jump ahead a minute at a time; skipping stops by itself when the next window opens.

How do I finish a level?
Get into a stable orbit around the target planet: close enough that its gravity holds you, but without skimming its surface. Brake by swiping along the red tick as you pass it.

I keep running out of fuel.
Choose Easy on the main menu for 20 km/s of fuel on every level. Burning at the window and correcting early, while you're far from planets, saves the most.

There's no sound.
Check the speaker button on the main menu, and make sure your iPhone's silent switch is off and the volume is up.

How do I reset my scores?
Delete the app and install it again. Your progress is stored only on your device.
